Erkence

also called Hurma zeytin (the debittered fruit, not a separate variety), furma in Karaburun

On certain trees of İzmir’s western peninsula, Erkence fruit loses its bitterness while still hanging on the branch and becomes edible with no brine, no lye and no curing at all. The olives are called hurma, and why it happens is still not settled.
