Coratina

also called Cima di Corato, Coratese, Racemo di Corato

The reference high-phenol Italian variety, and the one whose phenol load has been shown to translate directly into shelf life. In a same-site, same-mill comparison of eleven cultivars, Coratina measured 29.5 hours of oxidative stability against Leccino’s 17.5.
