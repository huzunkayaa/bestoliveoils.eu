Galega Vulgar

also called Galega, Galega miúda, Molar, Negrucha

Portugal’s dominant traditional variety by tree count — about 60% of the country’s olives — and one of its lowest yielding. Under 18% oil from fruit weighing less than two grams is precisely why it is being replaced in new intensive plantings.
