Cornicabra

also called Cornezuelo, Ornal, Cornicabra Negra

The variety of the central Spanish plateau, and the highest-phenol of the big four Spanish cultivars in the one study that measured them side by side. It has stayed home: adapted to cold, dry, poor soils, it has essentially no commercial presence outside Spain.
