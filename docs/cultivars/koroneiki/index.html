Koroneiki

also called Koroni, Kritikia, Ladolia, Psilolia

A very small fruit with a disproportionately high and stable oil yield — the combination that made Koroneiki the only Greek variety adopted at scale in mechanised hedgerow orchards around the world. It covers 50 to 60% of Greece’s olive area.
