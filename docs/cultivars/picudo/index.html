Picudo

also called Picuda, Picuo

The soft, aromatic counterweight in the great Córdoba blends. Picudo is what gives DOP Priego de Córdoba and DOP Baena their sweet entry — and its pollen quality makes it a standard pollinator in mixed Andalusian orchards.
