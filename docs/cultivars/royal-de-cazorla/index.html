Royal de Cazorla

also called Royal (do not confuse with Royal de Calatayud)

A mountain variety that survives as a six per cent minority inside a Picual monoculture, kept alive by its aroma rather than its yield. Royal gives less oil than Picual and measures higher in volatile compounds than any of the major Spanish cultivars.
