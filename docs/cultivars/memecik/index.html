Memecik

also called Aşı Yeli, Gülümbe, Şehir, Tekir, Yağlık

Türkiye’s leading oil cultivar and the most assertive of them on the palate — published Turkish panel work puts it at the top of both the bitterness and the pungency scale. It is also highly drought resistant, which matters more every year in the southern Aegean.

Published panel medians on a 10 cm scale (multi-region Turkish study): bitterness up to 5.5, pungency up to 6.1 — the highest recorded in that work. The IOC describes a balanced profile of fruity, bitter and pungent attributes.

Ordered by how often each recurs in the published varietal literature, not by measured intensity. Our panel has not scored a monovarietal Memecik oil, and no sensory panel data for it is published. 40 of the 40 varieties here read the same way.
