Tsounati

also called Athinolia, Mastoidis, Matsolia

The cold-hardy upland counterweight to Koroneiki, and an ancient variety in the literal sense — the IOC notes thousand-year-old Tsounati trees on Crete that are still in production. It holds 15 to 20% of Greece’s olive area.

Aroma and flavour descriptors

No descriptors are published for Tsounati oil. The IOC records only "oil quality: excellent". Greek sources describe a refined oil that blends well with Koroneiki, but no descriptor set or panel median is published. We do not infer them from the fruit or from related varieties — 40 of the 40 varieties here are in the same position.
