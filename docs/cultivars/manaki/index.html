Manaki

also called Manaky, often paired with Kothreiki

Chemically the opposite pole from Koroneiki: the lowest oleic acid and the highest linoleic of the four widely studied Greek cultivars. It is also a high-altitude variety, hardy to cold and wind, grown up to a thousand metres.
