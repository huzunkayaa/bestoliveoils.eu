Empeltre

also called Aragonesa, Injerto, Terra Alta, Mallorquina

The olive of the Ebro valley, and the one whose name is an agronomic fact: empeltre means "grafted" in Aragonese, because the variety will not root from cuttings in any practical way. Its oil is yellow, smooth and almost without bitterness.
