Moraiolo

also called Assisano, Morello, Morellino, Fosco

The hillside variety of central Italy: low-growing, upright, drought-tolerant and distinctly cold-sensitive — a combination that keeps it in the Umbrian and Tuscan hills and out of the flat coastal plantings. Its oil sits at the opposite end of the intensity scale from Leccino.
