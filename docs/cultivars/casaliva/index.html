Casaliva

also called Drizzar, Zentil, Casalì, Nostran

The northernmost commercially significant Italian oil variety, grown at the thermal limit of olive cultivation. Genetic fingerprinting shows it is the Garda name for the Frantoio genotype — and its oils are measurably lower in phenols than the same genotype grown further south.
