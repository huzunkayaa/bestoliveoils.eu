Verdeal Transmontana

also called Verdeal de Trás-os-Montes (distinct from plain "Verdeal")

The bitterness-and-pungency backbone of Trás-os-Montes blends, and the one Portuguese variety here with a directly measured Rancimat figure — 23 to 27 hours, roughly double a Madural from the same orchards and harvests.
