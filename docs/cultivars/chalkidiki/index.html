Chalkidiki

also called Chalkidikis, Chondrolia Chalkidikis, Gaidourelia, Prasinolia

A very large green table olive — six to twelve grams — whose early-harvest oil turns out to be among the more stable Greek monovarietals actually measured: 76 to 78% oleic acid with a Rancimat induction period up to 36 hours.
