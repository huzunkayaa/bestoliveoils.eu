Megaritiki

also called Megareitiki, Perachoritiki, Athinaiki, Elia Megaron

The chemical outlier among widespread Greek oil varieties — markedly lower in oleic acid and higher in palmitic than Koroneiki or Konservolia. Its oils do not simply taste less intense; they age differently.
