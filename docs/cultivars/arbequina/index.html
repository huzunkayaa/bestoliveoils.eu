Arbequina

also called Arbequí, Blancal

The variety that made modern hedgerow olive growing possible. Weak-growing, easy to root, quick to bear and self-fertile — and now planted on three continents, though it gives the least stable and lowest-phenol oil of the major Spanish cultivars.
