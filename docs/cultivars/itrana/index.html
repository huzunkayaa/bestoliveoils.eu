Itrana

also called Gaetana, Oliva di Gaeta, Oliva di Esperia, Trana

One variety, two harvests months apart: picked green in autumn it gives a high-oleic, green-tomato oil, and left to turn fully black it becomes the Oliva di Gaeta. It also carries real winter cold tolerance, which is rare in this company.
