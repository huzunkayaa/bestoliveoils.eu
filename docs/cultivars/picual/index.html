Picual

also called Marteño, Lopereño, Nevadillo Blanco

The most planted olive variety on earth and the backbone of Andalusian oil. Naturally rich in oleic acid and polyphenols, which makes it both the most stable extra virgin on the shelf and one of the most assertive on the palate.
