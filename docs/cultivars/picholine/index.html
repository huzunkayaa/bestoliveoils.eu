Picholine

also called Picholine du Languedoc, Coiasse, Collias, Olive de Nîmes

The reference French cocktail olive — lye-treated then brine-fermented for up to a year — and the sole principal variety of AOP Nîmes. Its name has been borrowed by an unrelated Moroccan cultivar that now dominates an entire national industry.
