Cobrançosa

also called Quebrançosa, Salgueira, Verdeal Cobrançosa

In a same-orchard, same-season comparison it produced roughly twice the total phenols of Galega — 803 against 416 mg/kg — at a lower oleic acid. It also roots easily and takes a trunk shaker, which is why it spread out of Trás-os-Montes into the Alentejo.
