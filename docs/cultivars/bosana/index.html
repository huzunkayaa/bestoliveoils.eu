Bosana

also called Sassarese, Tondo di Sassari, Algherese, Palma

The cultivar of an entire island — over half of Sardinian production — and the highest mean phenol content in Italy’s national monovarietal dataset. It is also, genetically, the same variety as Puglia’s Peranzana.
