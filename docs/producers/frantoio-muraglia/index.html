Frantoio Muraglia

A family mill in Andria now in its fifth generation under Savino Muraglia, known abroad for hand-painted ceramic jars and at home for its Coratina. The Essenza bottles — an intense Coratina, a medium Peranzana and a pitted Coratina — are the oils without the pottery. Founded year, hectares and trees are not published.

The estate

Coratina from the Andria plain, cold-extracted and filtered; the producer publishes a polyphenol figure for the Coratina (770 mg/L) and nothing for the Peranzana.

The ceramic jars are decorated by hand in Puglia and have made the brand a design object; the oil is the same as in the bottles.

Andria, Barletta-Andria-Trani, Puglia, Italy · 41.23° N, 16.29° E
