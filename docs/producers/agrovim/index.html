Agrovim — Iliada

The benchmark Kalamata, included as such: Agrovim continues A. Gyfteas S.A. of 1964, incorporated under its present name in 1994, and bottles Koroneiki from a network of Messinian growers at its own facility in Kalamata under PDO Kalamata. Iliada is the label most people outside Greece have met. It is a brand, not an estate — no hectares, trees or harvest window are published — and its medium-fruity, green-harvest profile is consistent from year to year. A 2025 NYIOOC Gold is reported through a retailer citing the company; Athena 2025 medals for the Iliada line are in the competition’s published results.
