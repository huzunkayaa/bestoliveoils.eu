Olearia San Giorgio

A family mill at San Giorgio Morgeto on the Piana di Gioia Tauro, run by the Fazari brothers since 1940, pressing the plain’s own olives — Ottobratica above all, with Carolea, Sinopolese, Roggianella and Ciciarello. The first Calabrian producer in the library.
