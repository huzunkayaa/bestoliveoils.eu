Frantoio Galantino

A mill on the coast at Bisceglie, between Trani and Molfetta, pressing Coratina from old groves inland and bottling a DOP Terre di Bari Castel del Monte line alongside its fruity-intensity range. Our partner stocks its children’s oils; the library carries L’Intenso. Founded year and estate size are not published.
