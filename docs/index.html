How the library works

What a listing here does and does not claim
1Nothing here is paid for

We do not charge a producer to be listed and we do not take submissions in exchange for coverage. An oil is here because we bought it, or because it placed in a competition we follow.

2Every claim names its source

A competition placing links to the competition. A polyphenol figure says who published it — the producer, an importer, or nobody. Where a number has no source, the page says so instead of printing one.

3A score means the panel tasted it

Most oils here are catalogue entries carrying a competition record and no score at all. Those pages say "our panel has not tasted this oil yet" and show no stars. We never fill the box with a guess.

4The shop is disclosed, not hidden

Some oils are stocked at olijfoliemarkt.nl and carry an "In our shop" badge. It is a disclosure, not a recommendation — nothing about a rating changes because an oil is or is not on the shelf.

Polyphenols & health

Why 250 mg/kg is the number that matters

EU Regulation 432/2012 permits an antioxidant health claim on an olive oil only where it carries at least 250 mg/kg of hydroxytyrosol and its derivatives. Below that figure the claim is not allowed, whatever else the label says.

We do not yet commission our own laboratory analysis. Where a producer or importer publishes a measured figure we print it and name who measured it; where nobody publishes one, the oil's page says exactly that rather than estimating.
