The gentler Pugliese olive, grown organically and bottled as a monocultivar under IGP Puglia. Ogliarola gives a soft, fruity oil — ripe olive, green herbs, a light pepper at the end — and here it carries, by the producer’s figure, 587 mg/kg polyphenols while staying smooth. The everyday bottle of the range for those who want the phenolics without Coratina’s bite.
