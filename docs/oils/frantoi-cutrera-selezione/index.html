Cutrera’s all-purpose oil: five native Sicilian varieties — Moresca, Biancolilla, Nocellara, Cerasuola and Tonda Iblea — from young groves near the coast, picked from early November. The later harvest gives a softer oil than the house’s early-picked Primo. Green with golden tints; artichoke, cut grass, green almond and red tomato on the nose, balanced bitterness and pungency, and a finish the producer describes as artichoke and oregano.
