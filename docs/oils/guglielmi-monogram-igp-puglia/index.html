Coratina in its purest form: a single-variety oil from the Andria plain, harvested and pressed within hours under the IGP Puglia designation, which requires every step from grove to bottle to happen in the region. Cut grass, artichoke and tomato, then the cultivar’s signature bitterness and a peppery finish that lasts. The producer states 527 mg/kg polyphenols — more than twice the EU 432/2012 threshold.
