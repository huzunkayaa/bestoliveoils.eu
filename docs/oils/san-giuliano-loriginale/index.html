The everyday oil of Sardinia’s best-known producer: Bosana, the island’s olive, with Coratina, Ogliarola, Carolea and Semidana from Italian groves, picked at late ripeness. Medium-intense — ripe olive, leaf and tomato, with light bitterness and pepper — by the producer’s description. Sold from 10 ml to a 3-litre tin.
