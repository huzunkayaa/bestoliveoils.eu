The premium bottling of a Baeza cooperative founded in 1951, whose 1,263 members work over half a million trees. Picked in early October while the fruit is still green. Joint-seventeenth in the World’s Best Olive Oils 2025/26.

Where it's from

Region map · Baeza, Jaén

Baeza is a UNESCO Renaissance town sitting on an ocean of olive trees. Cooperatives handle most of that fruit, and the premium lines are how they prove that co-op scale need not mean anonymous oil.
