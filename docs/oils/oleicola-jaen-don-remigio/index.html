Eighth in the World’s Best Olive Oils 2025/26. Don Remigio is not one oil but a premium line launched in January 2026, named after a co-founder of the company, with two monovarietal bottlings — Royal and Hojiblanca. The ranking does not say which of the two placed.

The facts

Where it's from

Region map · Baeza, Jaén

Royal is a Jaén native, grown almost nowhere else and notoriously hard to farm: low yields, fragile fruit, a short picking window. Bottling it as a monovarietal is a statement of intent rather than a commercial decision.
