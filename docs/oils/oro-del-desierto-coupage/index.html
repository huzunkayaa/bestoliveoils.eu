The estate’s most decorated oil since 2003: an organic blend of Arbequina, Hojiblanca and Picual whose proportions change with each harvest. Joint-thirty-first in the World’s Best Olive Oils 2025/26, and 99/100 in Flos Olei 2026.

Where it's from

The mill dates from 1925, built by the grandfather of the present owner, abandoned in the 1970s and restored. It now runs on photovoltaic power and composts its own pomace back into the groves.
