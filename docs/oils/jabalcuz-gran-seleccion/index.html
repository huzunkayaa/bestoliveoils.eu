Mountain Picual from the Sierra Sur de Jaén, picked in October and, the cooperative states, milled the same day. It took first prize in the medium green fruity class at the IOC’s Mario Solinas award in 2025.

Where it's from

Region map · Sierra Sur de Jaén

The Sierra de Jabalcuz rises straight out of the city of Jaén. The groves on its flanks are family-sized and hard to work, and the cooperative sells the result explicitly as mountain oil.
