An "extratemprano" Picual from a Baeza cooperative whose members work around 2,100 hectares on La Loma, and whose plant is sized so the whole day’s harvest is milled the same day. Joint-thirty-first in the World’s Best Olive Oils 2025/26.

The facts

CultivarPicual (retailer and press information; the co-op does not state it)
HarvestExtra-early, green fruit picked before véraison
PolyphenolsNot published
Free acidityNot published
MillThe day’s harvest is milled the same day; own bottling line
Cooperative~500 members, ~2,100 ha, 200,000–240,000 trees

Where it's from

Region map · La Loma, Jaén

The cooperative takes its name from its old site by Baeza’s watchtower, looking out over the Guadalquivir valley. It moved to a modern plant in 2004–05 but kept the name.
