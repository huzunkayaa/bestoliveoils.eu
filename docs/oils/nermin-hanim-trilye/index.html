Trilye is the Gemlik olive — Türkiye’s black table olive — under the name of the Mudanya village it is associated with. Picked early for oil it gives, by the producer’s label, an aromatic and balanced oil with high fruity notes. Sold filtered and unfiltered.

Where it's from

Gemlik is a Marmara olive; on the Edremit Gulf it is a minority planting, pressed here rather than brined.
