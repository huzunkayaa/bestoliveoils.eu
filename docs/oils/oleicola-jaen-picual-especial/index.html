The premium Picual of a Baeza mill that buys fruit from growers across the Jaén countryside and picks its premium lots green in October. Joint-thirteenth in the World’s Best Olive Oils 2025/26.

Where it's from

Region map · Baeza, Jaén

Baeza sits on La Loma, the ridge between the Guadalquivir and the Guadalimar. Jaén makes more olive oil than any country except Spain itself, and almost all of it is Picual.
