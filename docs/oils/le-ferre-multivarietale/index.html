The house blend for every day: Leccino and Ogliarola for softness, Frantoio and Coratina for structure. The producer labels it medium to delicate — fruity, a subtle bitterness, a pleasant spicy finish — and publishes 0.21% free acidity for the 2025/26 harvest with the lab sheet linked. Also sold in a 3-litre tin, which is the sensible format for an oil used this freely.
