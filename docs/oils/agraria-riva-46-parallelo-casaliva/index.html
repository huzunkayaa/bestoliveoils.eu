The northernmost olive oil in the library, from the co-operative at the head of Lake Garda — the 46th parallel of the name. 100% Casaliva, picked early and mostly by hand, cold-extracted on a two-phase continuous line. The producer describes a herbaceous, medium oil with balanced bitterness and pepper.
