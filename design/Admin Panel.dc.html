1. Keep it dark

Ultraviolet light breaks down chlorophyll and starts oxidation within days. A clear bottle on a sunny counter is the fastest way to ruin a good oil. Dark glass or tin, in a cupboard.

2. Keep it cool, not cold

Around 15–18 °C is ideal. The fridge is not: the oil clouds and thickens, and condensation gets in each time you open it.
